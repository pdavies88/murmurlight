Murmurlight

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

The short version: Murmurlight collects no data at all. It has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. All of its meditations and sounds are built into the app, so it never needs to connect to the internet.

Who this policy covers

Murmurlight is an iPhone and Android app made by Preston Davies, an independent developer. This policy explains how the app handles your information. If you have questions, email preston5@protonmail.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email, location or any other personal information. It does not record what you listen to, and it does not send anything from your phone to the developer or to anyone else.

Offline by design

Every guided meditation and soundscape is stored inside the app when you install it. The app works the same in airplane mode. It does not download content, check for updates on its own, or contact any server. App updates come only through the App Store or Google Play.

Permissions

  • Background audio. The app keeps playing when your screen is locked or you switch apps, so you can fall asleep to a sound. This uses your phone's standard media playback controls.
  • Notifications. If you turn on the bedtime reminder, the app asks to send notifications and schedules one gentle reminder each night, on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server. On Android, a playback notification with pause and stop buttons also shows while audio is playing. Turn the reminder off at any time on the Meditate tab.
  • Alarms. If you turn on Gentle wake, the app asks to set alarms and schedules one daily alarm on your phone. Turn it off at any time on the Meditate tab.
  • Apple Health. If you turn on Apple Health in the About tab, the app saves your meditation and breathing sessions to the Health app on your phone as mindful minutes. It only writes this one type and never reads any health data. This information goes only to Apple Health on your phone, never to the developer, and is never used for advertising. Turn it off in the About tab or in the Health app.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. The app tells Siri the names of your saved mixes, so you can ask for them by name. They stay on your phone.
  • Home screen widget and Live Activity. The widget shows the names of your saved mixes, and the Live Activity shows the sleep timer. Both read only what is stored on your phone.

The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.

App stores

Apple and Google may collect information when you download the app, such as crash reports you have chosen to share with developers. Their own privacy policies cover that. The developer does not receive any information that identifies you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Health information

The meditations are for relaxation and general wellbeing. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are dealing with a mental health or medical condition, please talk to a qualified professional.

Keeping and deleting data

The app collects nothing, so there is nothing to keep, share, delete or withdraw consent for. It saves only your sound mix, your named mixes, your sleep timer choice, your reminder and alarm times, and your sleep journal, on your phone. The journal keeps your nightly ratings and the sounds that played that night, so it can show which sounds help you sleep. None of this ever leaves your phone. You can delete a journal entry by touching and holding it. Deleting the app removes it and everything it saved from your phone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its effective date.
