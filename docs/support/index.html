Support

Need help? Email preston5@protonmail.com. I usually reply within a few days. The answers below cover the most common questions.

Is everything really free?

Yes. Every feature is free: the sound mixer, the sleep timer, the breathing pacer, Wind down and all 15 meditations. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything is built into the app, so it works in airplane mode, on a plane, or anywhere without signal.

How do I fall asleep to a sound?

Open the Sounds tab and tap one or more sounds, up to 4. They play together until you stop them, even with your phone locked. Drag the bar on each sound to set its volume. To stop after a while, choose a sleep timer: the mix fades out over the last minute. To stop now, tap the pause button on the mix card or on your lock screen.

What does Drift do?

Drift slowly raises and lowers the volume of each sound in your mix, each at its own pace. The mix keeps changing a little, like real rain or waves, so it never sounds like a loop.

How does Wind down work?

On the Meditate tab, tap Wind down. It runs two minutes of slow breathing, then a sleep meditation you choose, then your mix with the sleep timer. After the breathing you can lock your phone: the mix starts by itself when the meditation ends.

The sound stops when I lock my phone

  • Make sure your phone isn't in Low Power Mode with aggressive battery settings. On Android, check that battery optimization isn't restricting Lullmix (Settings → Apps → Lullmix → Battery → Unrestricted).
  • On Android 13 and later, allow notifications for the app. Android uses the playback notification to keep audio running in the background.
  • Another app that plays audio, such as a video or music app, can take over. Start the sound again after closing it.

I can't hear anything

  • Turn up your volume with the side buttons while a sound is playing.
  • On iPhone, the app plays even when the silent switch is on.
  • Check that your headphones or a Bluetooth speaker aren't connected to another device.

Can I use it for my child's naps?

Yes. Car Ride and Gentle Shush were made with little ones in mind, and Heartbeat and Brown Noise work well too. Keep the volume low, about as loud as a quiet shower, and place your phone a few feet away from the bed, not in the crib.

How long are the meditations?

Each of the five categories (Sleep, Stress Relief, Breathing, Body Scan and Focus) has a 3, 5 and 10 minute session. You can skip back or forward 15 seconds in the player.

Who made the meditations?

The meditation scripts were written for this app and narrated with an open-source Qwen3-TTS text-to-speech voice. The music box lullaby is Brahms' Lullaby, which is in the public domain. The guitar lullaby is an original tune. All music and sounds were synthesized for this app.
