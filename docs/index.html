Murmurlight

Mix your sleep sound. Drift off.

Murmurlight is a free sleep sound mixer with guided breathing and meditation. Everything is built into the app, so it works anywhere, even in airplane mode. There is no subscription and nothing to unlock.

  • Your own sound mix. Layer up to 4 of 19 sounds, such as rain, a crackling fire, singing bowls and binaural beats, and set the volume of each. Drift makes each sound slowly rise and fall. Your mix is saved on your phone.
  • A sleep timer that fades out. 15 to 60 minutes, with the countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Wind down in one tap. Two minutes of slow breathing, a sleep meditation, then your mix with the sleep timer.
  • Breathe. A pacer that grows and shrinks with your breath, with a gentle tap at each change.
  • 15 guided meditations. Sleep, Stress Relief, Breathing, Body Scan and Focus, each in 3, 5 and 10 minute sessions.
  • Completely private. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no internet needed.
